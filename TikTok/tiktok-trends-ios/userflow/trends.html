Trends Tab 📈

Phase 3 screen — tap any card or segment in the left phone to interact.

Bento Grid Layout

Cards alternate between:
• 1 wide featured card (full width, for #1 trend)
• 2 equal narrow cards
• 1 wide + 1 narrow
Keeps the grid feeling playful and asymmetric.

Card Types

Hashtag — keyword + velocity bar + flame rating

Music — cover art + artist + ▶ play button (AVPlayer)

Category — category name + velocity + view count

Trend Detail (right phone)

Pushed on card tap:
• Big keyword hero + stat tiles
• Top TikToks carousel (horizontal scroll, taps open TikTok)
• 7-day velocity sparkline
• Hot pink "Create with this trend" CTA

Interactions

• Pull-to-refresh updates trends
• Tap ▶ on music cards → inline AVPlayer preview
• Tap any card → push Trend Detail
• Tap "Create with trend" → switch to Create tab
